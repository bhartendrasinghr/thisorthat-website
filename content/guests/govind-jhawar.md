CA Govind Jhawar is the founder of Finideas Investment Advisor, a SEBI registered investment adviser. He has over 24 years of experience in financial markets and has trained more than 90,000 investors.

## What they care about

His investment philosophy is what he calls "Growth with Protection". Most investors go looking for whatever produces the highest return; Govind argues the honest measure is risk adjusted, which is to say not just how much you made but how much risk you carried to make it.

On ThisOrThat he explained how index investing can be paired with put options to limit the downside, where the put works like insurance: you pay a premium, and if the market falls hard you are covered, and if it does not, the premium was the cost of being covered. That runs straight into an uncomfortable fact, which is that SEBI data shows more than 90% of individual traders lose money in equity derivatives. His answer is that the instrument is not the problem, the intent is. Derivatives used for speculation and leverage magnify losses; used for hedging, they define and contain risk.

He also walked through a hypothetical one crore protected portfolio built from index ETFs, put options, futures and debt, including what the protection costs and why no strategy should be sold as risk free. The conversation went on to diversification, realistic return expectations, retirement, mutual funds against AIFs, and why investors who say they want to buy the dip so rarely do when the dip actually arrives.
