Bhautik Ambani is CEO and Director of AlphaGrep Mutual Fund, the fund house built out of AlphaGrep's quantitative trading business.

## What they care about

He came on to answer a question most fund houses would rather not put on a poster: why a fund can show strong returns while the people who own it earn less. The gap is not in the fund, it is in when people arrive and when they leave, so the conversation ran through investor behaviour, market cycles, and why picking a fund on its recent performance is the most reliable way to be disappointed by it.

The rest is about how the sausage is made. He explained quant investing in plain terms, how data actually gets used to build a portfolio and what can go wrong with a model, where human judgment still earns its place, and whether the Nifty is still the right yardstick. He also walked through AlphaGrep's multi-asset and flexi cap approach, what a SIF can do that a traditional mutual fund cannot, how long short strategies work, and where mutual funds, SIFs, PMS and AIFs each sit. It closes on the question everyone actually wants settled: SIP or lump sum.
