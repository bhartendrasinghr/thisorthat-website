Suranjana Borthakur is Head of Distribution at Mirae Asset Management.

## What they care about

She came on to separate two things most people treat as one: earning a return and actually building wealth. A fund can do well while the person holding it does not, because of when they arrive, what they chase and what they abandon.

The conversation covers asset allocation, what to do with a SIP when markets go nowhere, why yesterday's best performing asset is the wrong thing to buy, and how equity, debt and gold fit together in one portfolio. It also gets into rebalancing, lifecycle funds, SIFs and investing abroad, with the argument running through it that the right portfolio is decided by your goal and your horizon rather than by what did well last year.

The most candid moment is her own: during the COVID crash she was so heavily in equity that she could not take proper advantage of it. Being fully invested and being well positioned are not the same thing.
