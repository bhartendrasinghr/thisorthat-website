Vineet Malhotra is Co-Founder and CEO of compoundexpress, a DSP Group company. Before that he was a Managing Director at BlackRock.

## What they care about

He brings a perspective shaped by experience across the US, China, the Middle East and India, and uses it to explain how investing behaviour differs from one of those markets to the next. On ThisOrThat he argued that the future of wealth management is not AI against the financial adviser but the two working together, and that the hunt for the "best" fund is the wrong hunt. The better question is how to become a better investor: start early, invest against a goal, understand risk, diversify properly, and ask for help once your financial life gets too complex to manage alone.
