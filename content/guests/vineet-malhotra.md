Vineet Malhotra is Co-Founder and CEO of compoundexpress, a DSP Group company building software for financial intermediaries. Before that he was Head of Digital Business at DSP Mutual Fund, and a Managing Director at BlackRock, where he worked on product engineering and client technology across Asia Pacific.

## What they care about

He has invested and worked across the US, China, the Middle East and India, and he uses that spread to explain why investing behaviour is a product of the system people grow up inside rather than of how sensible they are. On ThisOrThat he argued that the future of advice is not AI against the adviser but the two together, and that the search for the "best" fund is the wrong search: the useful question is how to become a better investor, which means starting early, investing against a goal, understanding risk, and asking for help once your financial life gets too complicated to hold in your head.
