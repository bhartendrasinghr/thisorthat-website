Talk to a human

Some decisions need a person.

Tools are good at arithmetic and bad at your circumstances. Tell us roughly what you are working through and we will introduce you to someone qualified.

What we will ask

Enough to make a sensible introduction, and nothing more.

How this works
  • No commission. Koshli is not paid for the introduction.
  • No obligation. A conversation is a conversation.
  • Registered people only. SEBI-registered advisers and AMFI-registered distributors, and you can verify any of them yourself.
