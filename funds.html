Reading these numbers

Returns are past, not promise

Anything under a year is shown as a plain change, never annualised. Turning three months into a yearly figure is the commonest way these tools flatter a fund.

The worst twelve months is the honest one

Not the worst calendar year, the worst stretch from any starting day. It is what someone who bought at the wrong moment actually lived through.

Recovery time matters more than the fall

A 30% fall you sat through for eleven months is a different experience from the same fall that took four years to come back. Both are shown.
