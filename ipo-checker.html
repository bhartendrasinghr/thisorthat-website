Reading your own number

Three things worth knowing

Getting an allotment and buying on listing day are different investments

The issue price is what you paid if you were allotted shares. The listing-day close is the first price everybody else could get. On a hot IPO the gap between those two is most of the gain, and it goes to whoever got the allotment. That is why this page shows both.

The Nifty line is the honest comparison

A number on its own tells you nothing. Up 40% sounds good until the index did 60% over the same months. Every figure here is matched to the Nifty over the identical window.

Prices are adjusted for bonuses and splits

When a company issues bonus shares the price per share drops and your holding multiplies, so you have lost nothing. Raw prices make that look like a crash. These are corrected, and where a correction was applied the page says so.

This is history, not advice

It describes what has already happened to companies that have already listed. It says nothing about any IPO that has not opened yet, and it is not a view on whether you should apply to one. Picking an investment for you needs a registration we do not hold.
